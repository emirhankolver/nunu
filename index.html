FAQ

Questions, answered

How does Nunu AI work?

You pick a theme, add the photos or text it asks for, and our AI creates a set of images in that theme. Most sets are ready in a couple of minutes.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start right away as a guest. You can sign in later with Google or Apple to keep your creations across devices.

What are credits?

Each creation uses credits. If a creation fails for any reason, the credits are refunded automatically.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are used only to create your images. You can delete any creation in the app, which also deletes the photos you uploaded for it. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Can I use photos of other people?

Only if you have their permission. Please do not upload photos of children without a parent or guardian's consent. See our Terms of Use.

Which devices are supported?

Nunu AI is built for Android phones first. An iPhone version is planned.
