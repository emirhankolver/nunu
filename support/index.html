Frequently asked questions

How do credits work?

Each creation uses credits, and the cost is shown on the Generate button. If a creation fails, the credits are refunded automatically, and you can try again.

Which photos work best?

A clear, well-lit photo where the face (or the whole subject) is visible and sharp. For themes that need a full-body photo, stand straight and keep your whole body in the frame. Avoid sunglasses, heavy filters and very small faces.

Can I use a screenshot?

Yes. Themes that ask for clothes, products or rooms let you crop the photo after you pick it, so you can keep only the T-shirt, the trousers or the product.

My result does not look right. What can I do?

AI can miss details. Try again with a clearer photo, change the options (such as style or outfit), or pick a different theme. Results differ every time.

How do I save or share my images?

Open a creation and tap Save or Share. You choose which images to include. Themes that support video also offer Save as video.

How do I delete my photos?

Open the creation and tap the delete icon. This removes the generated images and the photos you uploaded for it. To delete your whole account, email us (see below).

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions and refunds are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Open your store's subscriptions page to cancel or to ask for a refund.

Something is not working.

Check your internet connection and update the app to the latest version. If it still fails, email us with what you were doing and your device model.
