Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Your photos are personal. This page explains in plain language what we collect, why, who helps us handle it, and how you can delete it.

1. Who we are

Nunu AI (the "app") is operated by Turnaworks ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal data we handle when you use the app and this website, why, and what choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Account data. You can use the app as a guest. We create a random device identifier so that you keep your creations and credits. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we also receive the name and email address that provider shares with us.
  • Content you submit. The photos you choose, the text, numbers and options you enter for a theme.
  • Content we create for you. The images, and the music track where a theme has one, that the app generates from your input.
  • Purchase information. If you buy credits or a subscription, the app store (Google Play or the App Store) handles payment and RevenueCat manages the purchase for us. We receive the purchase status and an app user ID, not your payment card details.
  • Technical data. Basic information sent with every request, such as your approximate country (derived from your connection), device and app version, and server logs used to keep the service running and to find problems.
  • Usage and analytics data. Which screens and features you use, app opens, device model, operating system and app version, and identifiers such as a Firebase installation ID and your device's advertising ID, collected through Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects the error details and the state of the device at that moment.
  • Advertising and install data. The Facebook SDK (Meta) records app installs and some app events, and can use device and advertising identifiers, so we can measure how well our ads work.

3. How we use it

  • To create your images and music, and show them to you in the app.
  • To manage your credits, refunds for failed creations, and subscriptions.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems, including by analyzing usage and crash reports.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.
  • To answer your support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your photos for advertising or analytics.

4. AI processing

To create your images, your photos and options are sent securely from our servers to an AI image generation provider (currently OpenAI). They are used to produce your result. We do not use your photos to train our own models. The provider handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy, and we ask you to review them.

AI results can be unexpected or inaccurate, and may not be unique. Please read our Terms of Use.

If we ever merge with or are acquired by another company, your data may move with the service, and we will tell you if that changes how it is handled.

Analytics, crash reports and advertising measurement

Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and the Facebook SDK collect the usage, crash and install data described above. We use it to understand and improve the app and to measure our advertising, not to read your photos. Where the law requires your consent for this (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom), we ask for it, and you can withdraw it at any time. You can also limit ad tracking or reset your advertising ID in your device's settings, or contact us to opt out.

6. Storage and retention

  • Your photos and creations are stored until you delete them. Deleting a creation in the app removes the generated images and the photos you uploaded for it.
  • Deleting your account removes all of your creations and the photos that belong to them. See "Your choices and rights" to request it.
  • Server logs and security records are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the service, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Purchase records may be kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Copies in our backups and caches disappear on a regular schedule after deletion.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a creation any time from its page in the app.
  • Delete your account and data by emailing support@turnaworks.com from the email address linked to your account, or if you are a guest, tell us your account details shown in the app so we can find it. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Access, correct or export your data, or object to how we use it, by contacting us.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Türkiye), you have rights under data protection law such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, if higher). We do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

9. Photos of other people

Only upload photos of people who have agreed to it. Do not upload photos of children unless you are their parent or guardian. Some themes ask you to confirm that the people in the photos are adults.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and private storage for uploaded photos. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and fix problems quickly.

11. International transfers

Our servers and database run in Germany (Hetzner), and some of our providers (such as OpenAI, Amazon Web Services, Google and Meta) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to protect it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. When we make an important change, we will update the date at the top and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@turnaworks.com or visit our support page.
